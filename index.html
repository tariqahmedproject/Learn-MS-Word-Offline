Privacy Policy — Learn Ms Word Offline

Last updated: 10 October 2026
App: Learn Ms Word Offline (com.LearnMsWordOffline)
Developer: Tariq Ahmed — tariqktk2015@gmail.com

Learn Ms Word Offline is an offline-first learning app. This policy explains what information the app and its service providers handle.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following information to operate, improve, and monetize the app:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID.
  • App usage (analytics): how you use the app — for example, which lessons you open, quiz and exam progress, and crash/diagnostic data that helps us fix problems.
  • Advertising data: information used to show ads, such as your advertising ID and interactions with ads.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying personal information, except the certificate name you type yourself (see section 4).

2. How we use the information

  • Analytics — to understand how the app is used and improve lessons, quizzes, and the exam (e.g. via Google Analytics for Firebase).
  • Advertising — to display ads and measure their performance (e.g. via Google AdMob). Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID.
  • App functionality — daily learning reminders and core course features.

3. Sharing with third parties

We share the information described above with our analytics and advertising service providers (such as Google) so they can provide their services. These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information.

4. Information stored only on your device

The following never leave your phone and are stored only in the app's local storage:

  • Lesson progress, quiz results, and certificate exam progress/answers/scores
  • Your dark-mode and reminder preferences
  • The name you type for your certificate (used only to print it on the certificate)

5. Sharing your certificate

If you choose Save or Share via WhatsApp on your certificate, the certificate image/PDF is saved to your device gallery or sent to the app you select (e.g. WhatsApp). This sharing is entirely your own action.

6. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: you can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization), or reset your advertising ID there.
  • Notifications: daily reminders can be turned off anytime in the app's Settings screen or in your device notification settings.

7. Permissions

The app may request notification permission (for daily learning reminders). No other special device permissions are required.

8. Children

The app is designed to be safe for all ages: no in-app purchases and no harmful content. Advertising shown to children is handled according to our ad provider's families policies.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
